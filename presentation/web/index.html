Its Flexible Combined Imager measures 16 spectral channels.

03 · THE AI MODEL

StormScope-EU predicts the next two hours of clouds

A generative diffusion model from NVIDIA. It forecasts all 16 satellite channels directly in image space, learning how clouds move and grow instead of solving the physics.
