02 · THE EYE IN ORBIT

Meteosat-12 sees Europe every 10 minutes

The first imager of EUMETSAT's Meteosat Third Generation. It sits 36,000 km above the Gulf of Guinea at 0° longitude and has been Europe's prime geostationary weather satellite since 2025.

03 · THE AI MODEL

StormScope-EU predicts the next two hours of clouds

A generative diffusion model from NVIDIA. It forecasts all 16 satellite channels directly in image space, learning how clouds move and grow instead of solving the physics.

06 · THE DATA LAKE

Every forecast run is a commit

Each 10-minute step uploads to Arraylake while the GPU computes the next one, and a single versioned Icechunk commit publishes all 12 at once. Readers never see a half-written forecast.
