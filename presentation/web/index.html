01 · What Is Nowcasting?

What Is Nowcasting?

Nowcasting resolves local atmospheric conditions from the present to six hours ahead, using rapidly updated observations to constrain the initial state.

Lagrangian advection, statistical learning, and numerical-model blending predict evolving structures. Convective initiation, growth, and decay challenge simple extrapolation.

03 · Generative AI Nowcasting

Predict the Next Satellite Observation

StormScope-EU conditions generative diffusion on six consecutive observations across 16 FCI channels. It predicts one multispectral frame at a time.

Each prediction enters the sliding context window. Twelve autoregressive steps extend the forecast two hours from initialization.

16 FCI channels per frame · Schematic, not inference telemetry
04 · NVIDIA Earth2Studio

New Observations Restart the Forecast Cycle

NVIDIA Earth2Studio connects observation retrieval, model initialization, GPU inference, and forecast output.

A newer complete six-frame observation window triggers the next cycle. Each initialization produces twelve 10-minute predictions; outputs stream to Arraylake during inference.
