01 · What Is Nowcasting?

Nowcasting predicts short-term atmospheric evolution, from minutes to a few hours, using recent observations and rapidly updated models.

This demo shows an end-to-end AI nowcasting workflow that transforms Meteosat satellite observations into two-hour forecasts of multispectral satellite imagery using NVIDIA StormScope-EU.

In this demo, we use the NVIDIA StormScope-EU model to generate the predictions, Earth2Studio connects the scientific workflow, and Earthmover enables low-latency forecast publication and interactive data access.

A forecast starts from an initial condition derived from recent observations.

02 · Earth Observations

Observing the Atmosphere from Space

Approximately 36,000 km above Earth, EUMETSAT’s Meteosat-12 observes Europe and surrounding regions.

Its Flexible Combined Imager (FCI) measures reflected solar and emitted thermal radiation across 16 spectral channels. Six consecutive observations provide the initial condition for StormScope-EU.

03 · NVIDIA StormScope-EU

Predicting the Next Satellite Observation

StormScope-EU learns spatiotemporal relationships across 16 satellite channels. Conditioned on six consecutive observations, it generates the next multispectral frame through iterative diffusion sampling.

Each prediction replaces the oldest frame in the sliding context window. Repeating this process produces twelve successive 10-minute predictions, extending the forecast to +120 minutes.

Predicted frames progressively enter the context during autoregressive rollout.
04 · NVIDIA Earth2Studio

New Observations. New Forecast Cycles.

Earth2Studio provides reusable components for retrieving observations, preparing model inputs, executing StormScope-EU on NVIDIA GPUs, and writing forecast outputs.

When a newer complete observation window becomes available, the workflow initializes another forecast. Each cycle incorporates updated observations and streams predictions to Arraylake during inference.

The Forecast Cycle
New observation windows enable new initializations; scan cadence and publication cadence are distinct.
05 · Earthmover Arraylake + Icechunk

Upload While Computing. Publish Atomically.

Forecast outputs move to Arraylake while the GPU computes subsequent predictions, overlapping data transfer with inference.

After every write finishes, one atomic Icechunk commit publishes the complete forecast as a consistent, versioned snapshot. Earlier snapshots remain available for reproducible analysis.
